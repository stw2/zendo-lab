One full public dev run per arm; no training or sealed evaluation. Unknown training corpora preclude a contamination-free claim. High effort is model-specific, not equal compute. Claude receives one fixed SDK identity sentence in addition to the benchmark prompts; sampling, output caps, provider serving and CLI behavior differ from E3. Model IDs are provider names rather than immutable weight snapshots. Malformed and capped complete replies were retained, never resampled. Comparisons are descriptive; the 20 individual 95% paired intervals have no multiple-comparison adjustment. Intervals spanning zero do not establish equivalence. Raw prompts, exposed reasoning and transcripts remain restricted.
